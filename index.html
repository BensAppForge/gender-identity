Historical Context & Referral Patterns

Prior to 2010, many clinics reported lower annual referral counts and different age distributions. Since then, pediatric referrals in several countries have increased. These figures describe service use and cannot, on their own, establish prevalence in the wider population.

Changes in AFAB/AMAB Distribution

Several published cohorts report a change in AFAB/AMAB distribution over time. Reported ratios vary by clinic, age range, and referral criteria.

Age Distribution in Referrals

In the datasets summarized here, referrals are concentrated in the adolescent years. This pattern may differ by period, region, and service structure.

Co-Occurring Clinical Indicators

Research, including the Cass Review (2024), reports that some teenagers seeking gender-related care also present with neurodevelopmental and mental health concerns. Reported rates differ across studies and should be interpreted in context of study design and sampling methods.

Neurodivergence: Studies indicate up to 35% of adolescent patients show traits of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD).
Mental Health: Over 60% of patients report significant pre-existing anxiety or depressive disorders.
